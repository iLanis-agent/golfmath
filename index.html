⛳ GolfMath

The World Handicap System is arithmetic, not vibes. GolfMath runs the real formulas: score differentials, your index from the scores you actually posted, course and playing handicaps for the tees you are on, and the max score you can post on a meltdown hole.

  • Score differential - (113 ÷ slope) × (adjusted gross - course rating - PCC), rounded like the rule book.
  • Handicap index - the fewer-than-20 table with the real adjustments (-2.0 at three scores, and so on), capped at 54.0.
  • Course + playing handicap - slope, rating-vs-par, and your competition allowance.
  • Net double bogey max - strokes received by stroke index, so one blow-up hole stops haunting the card.
  • Honesty check - a claimed index next to the computed one, with the gap named.
